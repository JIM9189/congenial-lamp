# Behind the Numbers

*Notes on audit, accounting, and the work of getting things right.*

---

Every set of financial statements is a story someone has chosen to tell.
The auditor's job is to ask whether it is true.

I spent seven years asking that question: from 2017 to 2024 in EY's UK
Assurance practice, rising to Senior Auditor, and qualifying as a
Chartered Accountant with ICAS along the way. This repository is where I
set down what that work taught me: the technical craft, the judgement
calls, the late nights, and the small moments that never make it into a
textbook.

## What lives here

- **The Craft:** standards, frameworks, and technique, explained plainly
- **The Toolkit:** checklists and templates that earn their keep
- **Field Notes:** lessons from the engagement room, told honestly
- **The Journey:** how a career in audit begins, builds, and changes

## For whom

For trainees finding their feet, for the curious weighing a path into
assurance, and for anyone who believes the quiet work of scrutiny
matters.
